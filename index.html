Wiring (Pico 2 pinout)

  1. T0 (physical action: switch / photodiode+comparator) → GP2 = pin 4  ■
  2. T1_CLICK (CH32V307 click output) → GP3 = pin 5  ■
  3. T1_MOTION (CH32V307 motion output) → GP4 = pin 6  ■
  4. GND common between the boards → pin 3 (near GP2) or 8 (near GP3/GP4).
  5. USB → the Pico 2’s own USB port (WebSerial/CDC).
  6. LED GP25 (on-board): blinks every 500 ms while the firmware is alive.
  7. Signals are 3.3 V — do NOT apply 5 V to the pins.

Measurement configuration

Free combinations: any T0 pull × edge × axis. A warning will appear here when the combination is unusual.
